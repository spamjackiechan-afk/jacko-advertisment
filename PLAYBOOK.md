## 3. Before-and-after photos (free samples and client work)
- Model: Higgsfield `gpt_image_2_6`, quality high, 2k, reference = the client's own photo (~2.75 credits).
- **Strict no-additions rule:** change only lighting, colour, cleanliness and the condition of existing surfaces. Do not add, remove or move furniture, plants, decor, objects, people, rooms or architectural features. Keep the same camera angle.
- After generating, compare against the original item by item; redo anything that added something.
- Present as: Before (left, dark label) / After (right, gold label), same size, original photo not upscaled.
- Always label publicly as an **AI-enhanced concept**.

## 4. Walkthrough videos
- Style: **door-to-door first-person walkthrough** (the camera walks up, the door swings open, it steps into the next room), like the fictional coastal motel example on the site.
- **No hands, arms or people** — doors open on their own. Check every frame; cut or re-run anything that shows a person or hand.
- Real properties: build from the property's own photos, retouched with the no-additions rule. Between-photo walking invents space, so for real places prefer room-by-room push-ins joined with door-style transitions, unless Jackson approves door-to-door.
- Fictional properties: allowed only when clearly labelled "fictional property · AI-generated".
- Model: Seedance 1.5 Pro, 1080p, no audio (4 s ≈ 12 credits, 8 s ≈ 24 credits). Compress for the web (H.264, ~1280 px wide, faststart).
